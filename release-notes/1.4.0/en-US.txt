A quieter, more editorial Daymark.

• New look: paper and ink with serif dates and easy-to-read type, so your photos are the colour on the page.
• Each marked day now gets a postmark with its place and date, stamped with a gentle haptic.
• Months open with a large numeral and a count of the days you marked.
• A new tab bar, greyscale maps and wider, evenly spaced photo grids.
• Photos are stored more compactly, which saves space and makes iCloud sync smoother. Existing photos are converted once after the update.
• Fixed crashes when deleting a photo and when exporting a backup.
