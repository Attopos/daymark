Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 2026

Daymark respects your privacy.

Information stored by the app may include:

  • Photos selected by the user
  • Dates associated with entries
  • Optional location information

Data is stored locally on the user's device and may be synchronized through the user's personal iCloud account when iCloud sync is enabled.

Daymark does not:

  • Sell personal information
  • Share personal information with advertisers
  • Track users across apps or websites
  • Use third-party advertising SDKs

The developer cannot access users' private photos, entries, or location data stored in their personal iCloud account.

Users may delete their data at any time by removing content from the app or disabling iCloud synchronization.
