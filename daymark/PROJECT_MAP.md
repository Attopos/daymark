# Project Map: Daymark

## Purpose
Traditional photo apps like "Apple Photos" or "Google Photos" storage your memories unevenly: some day you had 73 photos, some day 3 some day 145. This will not help your remember the "less shining days" and form a consistant narritive of your life. Further more, even though there are functions like "Places" in Google Photos and "Trips" in Apple Photos, you still need additional steps to check where was a photo shot, especially for international travelers.
Daymark fixs them all. "What represents today?" is the key idea of this project. You can add one, and ONLY one photo to mark a day (that's why it is called "Daymark"). What's more, we automatically add the location of the photo and show it right away. You can also use map view or timeline view to see how precious days and memories have lead to your current self.
